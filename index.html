About the Project

Multimodal biosignal fusion is an innovative approach that combines different types of physiological signals to provide a more comprehensive understanding of patient health status. This project integrates ECG (Electrocardiogram) signals from the heart and EEG (Electroencephalogram) signals from the brain.

ECG and EEG signals are preprocessed to remove noise and artifacts, followed by feature extraction using deep learning models such as Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks. A multimodal fusion strategy is then applied to integrate features from both signals for classification tasks.

The proposed system demonstrates improved diagnostic performance compared to single-modality approaches and can be applied in real-time health monitoring, early disease detection, and smart healthcare systems.

Why Multimodal?

✓

Reduces Uncertainty

Multiple signal sources provide redundancy and cross-validation, reducing diagnostic uncertainty.

↗

Improves Robustness

The system remains effective even if one signal type is compromised or unavailable.

▥

Enhances Accuracy

Combined analysis of cardiac and brain signals captures complex physiological patterns.

Clinical Applications

Conditions like stress, epilepsy, and cardiac arrhythmias often involve both brain and heart responses. By analyzing ECG and EEG together, this system provides a more holistic view of patient health.

Problem Statement

Single biosignal systems (ECG-only or EEG-only) have limited diagnostic capability and may miss critical cross-domain patterns.

There is a critical need for combined multimodal analysis to enable better healthcare decisions and early intervention.

Model Architecture

Hybrid CNN-LSTM Architecture for Multimodal Feature Extraction

ECG Pipeline (187 features)

CNN Layer 1 64 filters (Conv1D) + Batch Normalization Activation: ReLU | MaxPooling: 2
CNN Layer 2 128 filters (Conv1D) Activation: ReLU | MaxPooling: 2
LSTM Layer 64 units - Temporal learning Activation: Tanh

EEG Pipeline (4097 features)

CNN Layer 1 64 filters (Conv1D) + Batch Normalization Activation: ReLU | MaxPooling: 2
CNN Layer 2 128 filters (Conv1D) Activation: ReLU | MaxPooling: 2
LSTM Layer 64 units - Temporal learning Activation: Tanh
Fusion Layer Concatenation of ECG + EEG features
Dense Layer 1: 128 neurons + Dropout (0.3) | ReLU
Dense Layer 2: 64 neurons | ReLU
Output Layer: 2/5 neurons | Softmax Classification: Normal / Abnormal (or 5-class)

Conclusion

✓

A multimodal deep learning-based health monitoring system was successfully developed by integrating ECG and EEG signals . The hybrid CNN-LSTM model effectively extracts and fuses features from both biosignals.

✓

The model achieved a validation accuracy of 97.12% , demonstrating strong performance and good generalization. Techniques such as Dropout and Batch Normalization helped prevent overfitting.

✓

A user-friendly GUI was developed to allow real-time signal visualization and prediction . This project highlights the potential of multimodal biosignal fusion in building intelligent healthcare systems for early disease detection and continuous monitoring.
